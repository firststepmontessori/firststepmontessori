# Joyful Geometry theme

- Status: Approved and implemented
- Audience: School approver, designers and frontend developers
- Owner: Theme maintainer
- Last updated: 2026-08-30

## Character

Geometry presents the school as bright, contemporary and well organised. Friendly learning forms create energy without relying on photographs or cartoon characters. The design deliberately avoids brutalist borders, marquees, oversized shouting typography and constant motion.

## Shape system

Circles suggest focus and continuity; arches suggest passage and shelter; stepped squares suggest progression; half-moons and paired wings provide variation. Shapes use crisp fills and restrained two-pixel outlines, staying on a modular grid.

## Motion

Shapes settle, align or rotate no more than four degrees. Hover movement is capped at three pixels and 220ms. No element loops indefinitely. The approved concept board is in [`design/joyful-geometry-concept.png`](../design/joyful-geometry-concept.png).
