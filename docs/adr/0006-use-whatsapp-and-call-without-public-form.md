# ADR 0006: Use WhatsApp and call without a public form

- Status: Accepted
- Audience: School owner, privacy reviewer, content staff and developers
- Owner: Product owner
- Last updated: 2026-08-31

## Context

Parents need a fast contact path, but a public form would create storage, spam, consent, retention and response-management obligations.

## Decision

Provide direct WhatsApp, telephone, email, Instagram and map links. Do not provide an enquiry form or collect parent/child details on the website.

## Alternatives

A custom form and database would be technically possible but create sensitive-data handling. Third-party form tools expand processors and tracking risk. Publishing only an email address is less convenient on mobile.

## Consequences

The site stores no enquiries and needs no spam defense or enquiry-retention policy. Users leave the site for third-party communication services and are subject to those services’ privacy terms.
