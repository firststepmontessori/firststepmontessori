## Content ownership

The school approves school facts, programme names/ages, hours, team wording, admissions process and claims. The maintainer owns interface copy, accessibility text, technical policy wording and metadata implementation, subject to school review when it describes school operations.

## Verification rules

Facts are either verified, assumed/deferred, or explanatory. Verified facts may enter the default content model after written approval. Assumptions must be labelled in public copy or omitted. Explanatory Montessori content must avoid promising outcomes or implying accreditation.

## Navigation principles

Five secondary routes accompany the linked home logo. Admissions & Contact is both a route and the primary action. Privacy, child-safety and accessibility pages remain footer-only. Navigation structure cannot be edited in the admin interface, preventing broken information architecture.
