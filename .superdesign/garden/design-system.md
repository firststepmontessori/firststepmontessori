# First Step Montessori — Calm Prepared Garden

## Intent

A calm, prepared, welcoming Montessori environment expressed through warm paper-like surfaces, botanical line work and orderly wooden-block geometry. The design should feel trustworthy to parents and delightful to young learners without appearing childish, busy or corporate.

## Non-negotiables

- No photography, people, children, stock imagery or photo placeholders.
- Use only code-native CSS shapes, SVG line illustrations and typographic composition.
- No invented school claims, awards, affiliations, reviews or accreditations.
- Keep headlines and primary calls to action visible immediately; never animate their first paint.
- Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and obvious keyboard focus.

## Typography

- Display: Fraunces, softly expressive, sentence case, weight 600–700.
- Body and UI: Atkinson Hyperlegible, weight 400–700.
- Headlines are editorial and warm, not oversized display stunts.
- Avoid eyebrow labels above the main heading.

## Shape and illustration language

- Fine botanical SVG stems, leaves and seed forms.
- Ordered arch, cylinder and block motifs inspired by Montessori materials, without copying branded materials.
- Rounded corners from 16–28px, restrained one-pixel borders and almost no shadows.
- Plenty of negative space and an underlying precise grid.

## Motion

- Botanical strokes draw once as sections enter the viewport.
- Small blocks settle by 4–8px using opacity and transform only.
- Entrance duration 240–420ms; interaction duration 140–220ms.
- No parallax, marquee, scroll-jacking or constantly moving decoration.
- Remove nonessential motion for `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Page composition

- Compact sticky navigation with logo, six public routes, colour-mode control and an Admissions button.
- Hero: strong school name, concise value proposition, WhatsApp and call actions, location, and an abstract prepared-garden illustration.
- Programme pathway: Buds, Caterpillar, Cocoon and Butterfly with age bands clearly shown.
- Montessori approach, daycare, daily rhythm, location and closing visit CTA.
- Footer contains contact details plus privacy, child-safety/media and accessibility links.
