# First Step Montessori — Joyful Geometry

## Intent

A bright, contemporary Montessori identity built from friendly geometric learning forms. It should feel optimistic, structured and memorable while remaining calm enough for parents evaluating a school.

## Non-negotiables

- No photography, people, children, stock imagery or photo placeholders.
- Use only code-native CSS shapes, SVG illustrations and typographic composition.
- No invented school claims, awards, affiliations, reviews or accreditations.
- Keep headlines and primary calls to action visible immediately; never animate their first paint.
- Maintain WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and obvious keyboard focus.

## Typography

- Display: Nunito Sans, rounded but highly legible, weight 750–900.
- Body and UI: Atkinson Hyperlegible, weight 400–700.
- Use sentence case; avoid shouty all-uppercase headings.
- Avoid eyebrow labels above the main heading.

## Shape and illustration language

- Circles, arches, half-moons, rounded squares and small stepped block structures.
- Outlined and filled shapes share a consistent 2px visual weight.
- Asymmetric compositions stay within a disciplined modular grid.
- Corners range from 12–24px; avoid gradients, heavy shadows and aggressive brutalism.

## Motion

- Shapes stack, align or rotate by no more than 4 degrees once on entrance.
- Buttons and cards shift no more than 3px on interaction.
- Entrance duration 240–420ms; interaction duration 140–220ms.
- No marquee, infinite spin, flashing, scroll-jacking or constant distraction.
- Remove nonessential motion for `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce`.

## Page composition

- Floating but compact navigation with logo, six public routes, colour-mode control and an Admissions button.
- Hero: school name, clear value proposition, WhatsApp and call actions, location, and a structured geometric learning landscape.
- Programme pathway uses four distinct but harmonised shape families for Buds, Caterpillar, Cocoon and Butterfly.
- Montessori approach, daycare, daily rhythm, location and closing visit CTA.
- Footer contains contact details plus privacy, child-safety/media and accessibility links.
